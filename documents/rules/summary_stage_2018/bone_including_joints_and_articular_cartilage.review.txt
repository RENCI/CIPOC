    | ###### Note 5: Spinal tumors

    | are involved, this would be localized (code 1). Any other vertebral segments involved (non-adjacent) would be regional (code 2).

    | ###### Note 7: Definition of Regional lymph nodes

    | ###### Note 8: Lymph node involvement in bone is rare

    | - Regional lymph node involvement is rare.
    | - If there is no mention of lymph node involvement clinically, assume that lymph nodes

    | are negative.
