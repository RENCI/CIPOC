    | ###### Note 3: Bones of the lateral wall

    | - The bones of the lateral wall of the nasal cavity include the maxilla, the perpendicular

    | plate of the palatine bone, the medial pterygoid plate, the labyrinth and inferior concha of the ethmoid.

    | - The roof of the nasal cavity is formed by the nasal bone.
    | - The floor of the nasal cavity, which forms the roof of the mouth, is composed of the

    | bones of the hard palate

    | - The horizontal plate of the palatine bone posteriorly and the palatine process of the

    | maxilla anteriorly.

    | ###### Note 4: Bone involvement

    | - Involvement of or extension to bone includes any type of tumor extension to the bone,

    | such as erosion, invasion, extension, penetration, or destruction.

    | -----

    | ###### Note 5: Bony invasion

    | - **Does not include extension to palate, cribriform plate, or pterygoid plates.**

    | `o` Extension to these structures is coded separately.

    | - **Does include involvement of perpendicular plate of ethmoid bone or ethmoid air cells.**

    | ###### Note 6: Base of skull involvement

    | - Code 2 for base of skull, NOS when there is no information available for more specific

    | bony structures in the skull.

    | ###### Note 7: Extension to anterior cranial fossa

    | - Minimal extension to anterior cranial fossa implies tumor pushing through the

    | cribriform plate, but without invasion of the dura or brain.

    | - Ethmoid Sinus (C311)
